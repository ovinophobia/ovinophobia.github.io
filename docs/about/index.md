## About ovinophobia

The name *ovinophobia* came from one of my favourite game *Kingdom Rush*. There is an achievement with the same title in the game, and you need to kill 10 sheeps in the maps to get it. The word *ovino* origins from Spanish and Italian, meaning *sheep*. And the suffix *phobia* is commonly used in medicine, to describe the symptom where you are afraid of certain object or situation.

Therefore, if someone has an *ovinophobia*, it simply means that he is scared of sheep. Actually, I am not afraid of sheep. I just thought it very interesting and took it as the name.
